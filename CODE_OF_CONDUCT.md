# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, ability, or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Assume good faith while addressing concrete technical or safety concerns.
- Respect privacy and never post another person's mailbox data, credentials, or identifying information.
- Accept maintainer decisions about scope, security embargoes, and community safety.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, doxxing, impersonation, deliberate credential exposure, or sustained disruption are not acceptable in project spaces or when representing the project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, restrict participation, or ban contributors when behavior threatens people or the project. Enforcement decisions should be proportionate and protect reporter privacy.

Report conduct concerns privately through the maintainer contact options at [github.com/lanfuli](https://github.com/lanfuli). Use the security process only when the report also concerns a vulnerability. Do not include unnecessary personal or mailbox data.

This code applies to repository discussions, reviews, issues, pull requests, community spaces, and public representation of the project.
